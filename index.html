Syed Sadain Haider is the founder of TransitionAI and a computer science Ph.D. student at Virginia Tech. His research interests include multimodal foundation models, physical intelligence, embodied AI, vision-language-action models, agentic reasoning, and applications of artificial intelligence in education.

He previously worked as a senior software engineer at Datics AI. His engineering experience includes autonomous AI agents, multimodal video intelligence, information retrieval, conversational AI, and cloud-based software engineering.

Education

Syed began his Ph.D. in Computer Science at Virginia Tech in August 2026, where he also serves as a graduate teaching assistant for CS 2104: Introduction to Problem Solving.

He earned a Bachelor of Science in Software Engineering from the University of Management and Technology in 2025, graduating with a CGPA of 3.92/4.0 and ranking second among 290 students. He was a silver medalist and received the GreenAir Special Gold Medal, three Rector Gold Medals, three Dean Silver Medals, and a full merit scholarship with stipend.

His final-year thesis was titled SmartGrade AI: Agent-Driven Automated Exam Checking with Reasoning Enhanced Feedback.

Research

At Virginia Tech, his research projects, advised by Dr. Sehrish Basir, focus on:

  • Scientific laboratory intelligence: Multimodal foundation models and embodied agents for perception, reasoning, and action in scientific laboratory environments.
  • Video-language understanding: Multimodal AI for video-language understanding, temporal activity recognition, and automated pedagogical assessment using natural language processing and computer vision.

His earlier research experience at the University of Management and Technology includes:

  • Gaze Estimation using Deep Learning and its Applications in Predicting User Actions (ICOSST 2024).
  • Behind the Scenes: Frame-level Video Search using Captioning and Similarity Search.
  • Image and Video Steganography using Deep Learning.

Career

TransitionAI (current)

Syed is the founder of TransitionAI.

Datics AI (former role; started March 2025)

As a Senior Software Engineer, Syed developed autonomous AI agents for extracting structured and unstructured data about medical providers from more than 1,000 sources, achieving 88% extraction accuracy. The work included data validation, language-model reasoning, and ETL pipelines built with Python, Snowflake, and AWS RDS.

He also developed a freelancer automation system using Google ADK, LangChain, and LangGraph, as well as video intelligence systems combining real-time object detection, multi-object tracking, and semantic search using YOLO, BLIP, and FAISS.

Yellow Beam Technologies (May 2024–March 2025)

As a Software Engineer, he developed conversational AI systems for call-center automation and an intelligent GraphRAG information retrieval system for medical and patient-care facilities, with reported retrieval accuracy of 94%. His work also included deployment of a blockchain-based weather reporting system and support for cloud infrastructure and CI/CD workflows.

MotionCode (August 2022–April 2024)

As a Software Engineer, he developed a collaborative video annotation platform, a VM monitoring system, and a face-recognition system deployed on AWS SageMaker that could search millions of facial images and identify duplicates with 98% accuracy.
